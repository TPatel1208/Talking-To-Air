id: tropomi-no2-conus-seasonal
title: TROPOMI NO2 CONUS seasonal means and the December-to-February season
datasets:
  - TROPOMI_NO2_CONUS_SEASONAL
---
Each TROPOMI_NO2_CONUS_SEASONAL value is the mean over one season of the TROPOMI pixels with a qa_value of 0.75 or more, gridded at 0.01 degree over the contiguous United States only. The seasons are DJF (December to February), MAM, JJA and SON. DJF spans two years and is named by its December's year: DJF 2018 runs from 2018-12-01 to 2019-02-28, so a January or February date belongs to the season named for the previous year. It is not a daily value: describe it as that season's mean. A season's file appears about two months after the season ends.
