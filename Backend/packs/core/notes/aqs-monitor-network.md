EPA AQS has about 500 US monitors, with records from 1980 to the present. Hourly values exist for NO2, O3, CO and SO2; PM2.5 is reported every 3-6 days; daily values (aggregated) exist for every pollutant. A monitor can be registered in EPA's metadata and still have no submitted measurements for the requested pollutant and dates.
