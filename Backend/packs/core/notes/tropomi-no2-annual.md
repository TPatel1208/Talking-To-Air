---
id: tropomi-no2-annual
title: TROPOMI NO2 annual means are one value per year
datasets:
  - TROPOMI_NO2_ANNUAL
---
Each TROPOMI_NO2_ANNUAL value is the mean, from January 1 to December 31, of the TROPOMI pixels with a qa_value of 0.75 or more, gridded at 0.1 degree. It is not a daily value and says nothing about any one day, week or month: describe it as that year's mean. A year's file appears about three months after the year ends, so the current year has no annual value yet.
