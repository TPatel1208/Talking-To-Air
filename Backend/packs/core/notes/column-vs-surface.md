---
id: column-vs-surface
title: Satellite columns are not surface concentrations
quantities:
  - no2_tropospheric_column
  - o3_total_column
  - hcho_total_column
  - so2_total_column
---
A satellite column density (molecules/cm^2, or DU for ozone) and an EPA ground monitor's surface concentration (ppb, ug/m3) are different physical quantities. Report both units explicitly. Never state or imply the two measure the same thing: frame a comparison as two distinct measurements of the same event, not a single value.
